        DOG RULES

        No dogs in the national parkKeep the dogs out of the national park around Mt Wilson, including the Cathedral of Ferns loop at Cathedral Reserve.

        Leads on at Windyridge and Hillbilly. Bring poo bags: there are no bins at some stops.

        Before you leave

        Call Windyridge on (02) 4756 2019 to check they're open and ask which paths are easiest for Ann. Book Hillbilly on (02) 4567 0965.

        OFFLINE MAP

        Save the 3D aerial map along the whole route to this phone so it keeps working without reception. It's roughly 30–60 MB, so do it on Wi-Fi before you leave.

        ABOUT

        Drag to move, pinch to zoom, two fingers to tilt and turn. Tap a stage to fly to it. The plane button flies the whole day with commentary; GO starts the live co-pilot, which follows your location, reads out what's coming up and keeps the screen awake.
